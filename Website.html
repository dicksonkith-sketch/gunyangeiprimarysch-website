About the school

Committed to every learner

Gunyangei Primary School exists to give children in our community a safe, encouraging place to learn, grow in character, and build the skills they need for the future.

Our mission

To provide accessible, quality primary education that develops confident, capable and caring young people — grounded in strong values and prepared for the next stage of their education.

Our community

Located c/- New Tribe Mission, P.O. Box 1079, Goroka, in the Obura Wonenara District Development Authority area, Gunyangei serves families across the surrounding community with dedicated teaching staff and an active school administration.

Admissions

Enrolling your child

We welcome enquiries from families in our district. Here's how enrolment works.

  1. Contact the school office

    Reach out via the details below, or visit in person, to ask about places for the coming term.

  2. Submit required documents

    Provide the child's birth certificate, immunisation record, and previous school report (if transferring).

  3. Meet with school staff

    A short meeting with the head teacher or admin to confirm grade placement and answer any questions.

  4. Confirmation & orientation

    Once confirmed, your child is added to the school roster ahead of the new term.

What you'll need

  • Child's birth certificate or proof of age
  • Immunisation / health record
  • Previous school report (for transfers)
  • Guardian contact details
